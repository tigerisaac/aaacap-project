Will Shield work with my phone?

Shield is designed for eligible T-Mobile and Tello lines in the United States. It requires carrier call forwarding, an active data plan, and setup in your iPhone’s settings. A subscription is required; carrier charges may apply.

Shield only screens forwarded calls. It does not verify a caller’s identity, cannot stop every scam, and may stop a call you wanted. If you’re unsure, call back using a number you trust.
