Calls stopped ringing

Shield only receives calls that your carrier forwards to it. To return to ordinary calls, open Shield and choose Settings → Disconnect Shield. The guide walks through each step and asks you to confirm that calls and voicemail work.

If you can’t open the app on a T-Mobile or Tello line, dial each code below and press Call. Wait for the carrier’s confirmation after each one.

  • ##61# stops forwarding unanswered calls
  • ##67# stops forwarding busy or declined calls
  • ##62# stops forwarding when your phone is unreachable

Then restore the phone settings you changed during setup. On iPhone, go to Settings → Apps → Phone and set Screen Unknown Callers and Live Voicemail back to how you had them. Ask someone to call you to confirm that your phone rings and your carrier voicemail answers. For other carriers, contact your carrier or email us.

Delete your account

Open Shield and choose Settings → Delete account. If you haven’t finished setup, choose Delete account on the first screen. Shield asks you to restore ordinary calls first, because deleting the app or account does not change carrier forwarding.

Subscriptions

Apple handles payment. To cancel or change a subscription, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Deleting Shield or your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Refund requests go to Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What Shield can’t do

Shield doesn’t verify who is calling and can’t screen calls your carrier doesn’t forward. It can miss scams or stop a caller you wanted to hear from. If you’re unsure about a call, hang up and call back using a number you already trust.
